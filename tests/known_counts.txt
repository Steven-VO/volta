# Each line: the expected number of graphs (N-classes), then the arguments for
# volta.  Sources of the expected values:
#   [T]  numbers of N-classes computed independently, by a brute-force count
#        of the orbits of the normaliser on the connection sets (Burnside's
#        lemma, and a direct enumeration where that was cheap)
#   [G]  nauty's geng: with orbits of length 1 the N-classes are exactly the
#        isomorphism classes of graphs on k vertices (e.g. geng -tf -u 6 = 23)
#   [R]  classical Ramsey numbers: R(3,3) = 6, R(3,4) = 9, R(3,5) = 14,
#        R(4,4) = 18 (so the (r,s)-graphs exist on R(r,s)-1 vertices, not on
#        R(r,s)), Paley(17) and the unique (4,5)-graph on 24 vertices
#   [I]  identities between two filters (K3- and C4-free = girth >= 5;
#        K3-e-free = maximum degree 1) and complementation symmetry
#   [X]  counts cross-checked against independent naive implementations of
#        the filters and against nauty during development
#   [C]  well-known graphs: Petersen (5,5), Heawood (7,7), McGee (8,8,8,
#        two N-classes of one isomorphism class)
